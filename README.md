# Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) Report – Pharmacovigilance Case Study

This report presents an Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) case documented from a pharmacovigilance perspective. The patient, **Prakash Namdev Patil, aged 45 years**, was associated with **Sanjivni Clinic, Shirpur**. Amoxicillin was identified as the suspected medicine. According to the available case information, the patient developed **skin rash, itching, and dizziness** after starting amoxicillin. The medicine was reportedly started five days before the reported assessment, and the symptoms began approximately two days before the assessment. Paracetamol was also used as needed. Based on the available information, the causality assessment was recorded as **Probable**, indicating a possible relationship between amoxicillin and the reported adverse reactions. The reaction was classified as non-serious in the original report. However, the patient's recovery status and outcome were not documented, and details such as the dose, route of administration, treatment indication, and dechallenge response were unavailable. This case highlights the importance of identifying suspected adverse drug reactions, collecting complete patient and medication histories, assessing causality, documenting clinical outcomes, and reporting suspected reactions through the appropriate pharmacovigilance system. The purpose of this case study is to support drug safety awareness and demonstrate the role of ADR documentation in protecting patient health.

**Keywords:** Pharmacovigilance, Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR), Amoxicillin, Causality Assessment, Patient Safety, ADR Reporting, Drug Safety Monitoring.
